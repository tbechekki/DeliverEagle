# Brainstorming

## New App Ideas - List

1. **DeliverEagle** – A basic food-delivery app, like Uber Eats. Users browse restaurants, view menus, add items to a cart, place an order, and track its status.
2. **NinerChat** – A simple messaging app for chatting with friends one-on-one or in groups.
3. **ScoreBoard** – A sports scores app that shows live and recent scores for your favorite teams.
4. **StudySpot** – Find open, quiet study spaces on campus with check-ins showing how busy they are.
5. **CampusSwap** – A marketplace for students to buy, sell, or trade textbooks and dorm items.
6. **TripSplit** – Track shared expenses on a group trip and see who owes whom.

## Top 3 New App Ideas

1. DeliverEagle
2. NinerChat
3. ScoreBoard

## New App Ideas - Evaluate and Categorize

### 1. DeliverEagle
- **Description:** Browse restaurants, view menus, add items to a cart, place an order, and track its status.
- **Category:** Food & Drink / Lifestyle
- **Mobile:** Uses maps, location, and push notifications for order updates; ordering on the go is naturally mobile.
- **Story:** The value is instantly clear — get food without leaving your room. Friends already use apps like this.
- **Market:** Huge — anyone who orders takeout, especially college students near campus.
- **Habit:** Opened whenever users are hungry, often several times a week.
- **Scope:** Very buildable with mock restaurant data and a simulated checkout; still feels like a real app.

### 2. NinerChat
- **Description:** Send messages to friends one-on-one or in group chats.
- **Category:** Social Networking
- **Mobile:** Real-time messaging, push notifications, and camera for sending photos.
- **Story:** Easy to understand, but it competes with apps everyone already uses (iMessage, Snapchat).
- **Market:** Large, but hard to stand out without a unique twist.
- **Habit:** Very habit-forming — users open messaging apps many times a day and create content constantly.
- **Scope:** Hard — real-time messaging needs a backend, user accounts, and syncing between devices.

### 3. ScoreBoard
- **Description:** See live and recent scores for your favorite teams and leagues in one place.
- **Category:** Sports
- **Mobile:** Push notifications for game starts and final scores; real-time updates.
- **Story:** Clear value for sports fans, but similar to ESPN and other existing apps.
- **Market:** Large audience of sports fans.
- **Habit:** Opened daily during the season; mostly consumption, not creation.
- **Scope:** Moderate — depends on finding a free sports API, and live data can be limited or rate-limited.

## Final Decision

**DeliverEagle.** It has the clearest story, uses several mobile features, and has a well-defined MVP that can be built with mock data within the course timeline. NinerChat is too complex to finish in time, and ScoreBoard depends too much on an outside API.
